Who We Are

FlourishHub is a human development and empowerment platform committed to catalysing human flourishing. As the Centre for Human Flourishing, we work to equip individuals, strengthen communities and develop purpose-driven leaders through learning, mentorship, coaching, counselling, peacebuilding and empowerment initiatives.

We recognize that flourishing is holistic. It includes emotional and social well-being, meaningful relationships, practical skills, education, economic opportunity, leadership and the ability to contribute to the common good. Our programmes are designed to respond to these connected dimensions of human development.

We place particular emphasis on women and girls, children and young people, emerging leaders, and families and caregivers, especially those experiencing social, educational or economic barriers.

Our Vision

A flourishing society where individuals, families, communities and organizations thrive physically, emotionally, socially, spiritually and economically, creating positive and lasting change across generations.

Our Mission

To catalyse human flourishing by equipping people with the skills, knowledge, support, opportunities and resources they need to unlock their potential, strengthen their resilience, exercise leadership and create meaningful impact.

Our Purpose

To help people move from survival to flourishing by addressing personal, social, emotional, educational and economic barriers, while strengthening the capacities and relationships that enable individuals and communities to thrive.

What we aim to do

  • Empower women and girls with life skills, leadership opportunities, mentorship and pathways to social and economic well-being.
  • Support children, adolescents and young people through educational support, mentorship, personal development and positive life skills.
  • Promote peacebuilding, conflict transformation, dialogue, emotional intelligence and ethical leadership through the Peace and Leadership Academy.
  • Develop peace champions and responsible peer leaders through the Peace Ambassadors Programme and Peace Clubs in schools and communities.
  • Strengthen the well-being and resilience of individuals and families through coaching, counselling, psychosocial support and relationship skills.
  • Build the capacity of schools, community groups and organizations through leadership development, communication, public speaking, team building and customized training.
  • Foster inclusive partnerships and community-led initiatives that expand access to learning, empowerment and sustainable opportunities.
  • Encourage service, integrity, compassion and community participation in addressing social challenges and creating lasting positive change.

Who we work with

  • Schools, colleges and universities for student mentorship, life skills, peace clubs, leadership development and educational support.

Leadership

President, FlourishHub

Dr. Jackylene Wegoki Gitari

Dr. Jackylene Wegoki Gitari is an Executive Coach, Counsellor, Author, Peacebuilding Specialist and Human Flourishing Strategist committed to developing purpose-driven, resilient and values-based leaders. She holds a Professional Doctorate in Peace and Conflict Resolution and an MBA in Human Resource Management. She is a Registered Counsellor and a Certified Career Coach, Public Speaking Coach and Mental Health Coach.

Her professional experience spans executive coaching, leadership development, public speaking, organizational capacity building, partnership management, counselling and life skills development across corporate, public, faith-based and development settings. She is the host of The Flourishing Table and author of Rise and Flourish: From Survival to a Life of Abundance and Impact.

Her leadership philosophy connects peace, purpose, people and leadership. Through FlourishHub, she champions the belief that flourishing individuals can strengthen relationships, build resilient institutions and contribute to lasting community impact.

Professional background

  • Executive Coach and Trainer, African Centre for Public Speaking (ACPS), supporting professionals, leaders and institutions for over five years.
  • Capacity Building and Partnership Management Lead, HealthPartners International Development (USAID Kenya).
  • Lead Operations, Anglican Church of Kenya, responsible for staff capacity building, curriculum development, corporate communications and partnership management.
  • Consultancy and development sector work with Education For All Children (EFAC), Windle International and World University Service of Canada (WUSC) on mentorship, life skills training manuals, trainer capacity-building and resources for girls in Kenyan refugee camps.
  • Faith-based and community training, including volunteer lead trainer for Children’s Ministry leaders with Bible Centered Ministries (UK) and Global Children’s Forum (UK).

Accreditation, education and affiliations

  • Certified Career Coach, accredited by the Professional Association of Resume Writers and Career Coaches (PARWCC), USA, in partnership with CDI Africa.
  • Mental Health Coach, accredited by Light University, USA.
  • Registered Counsellor, Kenya Counsellors and Psychologists Board.
  • Education: Professional Doctorate in Peace and Conflict Resolution (Bilingual University, Togo); MBA in Human Resource Management (Moi University); Bachelor of Education, Home Economics (Kenyatta University).
  • Member of the Institute of Human Resource Management (IHRM), Kenya; SEMA Toastmasters Club; and the Institute of Peace and Conflict Resolution, Nigeria.
