Partner With Us

Human flourishing is a shared endeavour.

FlourishHub welcomes partnerships with schools, universities, corporates, foundations, donors, NGOs, public institutions, faith-based organizations, community groups and individuals who share our commitment to people-centred development.

Ways to partner

Programme sponsorship

Support the delivery of peacebuilding, youth leadership, women’s empowerment, life skills or educational initiatives.

School and community partnerships

Host Peace Clubs, Peace Ambassador training, mentorship and leadership activities.

Skills and career opportunities

Contribute trainers, mentors, digital learning, vocational exposure, internships or career guidance.

Corporate and institutional engagement

Commission leadership, communication, team effectiveness and customized capacity-building programmes.

Volunteer and mentor

Share professional expertise, support learning activities or mentor participants within a structured programme.

Funding and in-kind support

Contribute financial resources, learning materials, venues, equipment or other agreed programme inputs.

Programme design and learning

Collaborate on programme development, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and learning.
