Impact & Stories

Learning from the people and communities we serve.

FlourishHub is committed to learning from the people and communities it serves. We aim to document programme participation, learning, participant feedback and meaningful changes in skills, confidence, leadership, well-being and opportunity.

This page features only verified results and stories shared with informed consent. As programmes are delivered, we will share programme reach, participant learning and feedback, school and community partnerships, examples of Peace Club activities, participant stories, and lessons that have improved how we work.
