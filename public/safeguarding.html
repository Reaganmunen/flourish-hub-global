Safeguarding

Safe, inclusive and responsible programmes.

We work with children, young people and families, and we take our responsibility to keep them safe seriously.

Our approach

FlourishHub supports safe, inclusive and sustainable development for children, young people and families. Our programmes are designed with age-appropriate activities and clear roles, and we work with schools, communities and parents and caregivers to keep participants safe.

Peace Ambassadors and Peace Clubs

Peace Ambassadors are peer leaders. They are not substitutes for counsellors, safeguarding leads or trained mediators. Serious concerns must be referred through agreed school, community or professional channels.

Each Peace Club should have a designated adult patron or community focal person, and clear safeguarding and referral procedures.

Counselling and coaching

Coaching, counselling and mentoring are delivered within appropriate professional scope. Where another service is better placed to help, we refer people onward.

Photographs and stories

We obtain informed consent before publishing identifiable photographs, participant stories and testimonials, with particular care for children.

Raising a concern

If you have a safeguarding concern about any FlourishHub activity, please tell us through our contact page. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
