Privacy Notice

How we handle your information.

We collect only what we need to respond to you, process your orders and run your account.

What we collect

Enquiries and sign-ups. When you use our contact or partnership forms or join our mailing list, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, organization, email address, phone number and message, so that we can respond.

Accounts and orders. If you create an account or buy from our shop, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional) and, for physical books, your delivery address. Passwords are stored in a protected, hashed form.

Payments. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to enquiries, deliver the books and content you buy, manage your account, and send updates if you asked to receive them. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

This website stores small pieces of data in your browser to keep you signed in and to remember the contents of your shopping bag. We do not use them to track you across other websites.

Photographs, stories and testimonials

We publish identifiable photographs, participant stories and testimonials only with informed consent, with particular care for children.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates, by getting in touch through our contact page.
