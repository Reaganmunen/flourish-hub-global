About the founder

Nourishing people to flourish.

Jackylene Wegoki is the founder of Flourish Hub Global and the author of Rise and Flourish: From Survival to a Life of Abundance and Impact — a book, and a hub, built around one belief: that survival is a season, not a sentence.

Flourish Hub Global started as a simple question: what does it actually take to move from surviving to flourishing? Jackylene's own answer to that question became Rise and Flourish — a book written for anyone standing at that same edge, deciding whether to stay where it's familiar or step toward abundance and impact.

This hub carries that same mission forward in three forms. The shop holds the book itself, in print and digital editions. The Journal holds essays and reflections on growth, resilience, and purpose — some open to everyone, some reserved for members. The podcast holds the conversations that don't fit neatly into a chapter: unscripted, and sometimes unfinished on purpose.
